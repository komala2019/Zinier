02 · Problem and opportunity

Fragmented evidence creates avoidable cost

Today

  • Job exceptions shared across calls and messages.
  • Evidence arrives late or is incomplete.
  • Variation claims are difficult to verify.
  • Disputes delay payment and consume operations time.

Opportunity

  • Capture evidence at the point of work.
  • Give both parties visibility into job status.
  • Route exceptions through a consistent review process.
  • Make payment decisions faster and auditable.
03 · Users and needs

Design for both sides of the network

Operator teams

Regional managers need capacity and quality visibility. Vendor managers need evidence-backed decisions and a manageable review queue.

Console: performance + request review

Subcontractors and technicians

Owners need fair, predictable payment. Dispatchers need simple coordination. Technicians need fast, reliable workflows in poor-signal conditions.

Mobile: jobs + evidence + exceptions
04 · Product experience

Two simple product surfaces

Technician mobile app

A job in five steps

  1. Sign in with SMS and OTP.
  2. View assigned jobs and SLA details.
  3. Capture required photos.
  4. Raise a site exception or variation.
  5. Submit now or save offline and sync later.
Operator web console

See work and resolve exceptions

  1. Monitor job, SLA and first-time-fix metrics.
  2. Review requests by urgency.
  3. Compare dispatched scope, claim and evidence.
  4. Approve, modify or reject with a reason.
  5. Track decisions and audit history.
05 · Core requirements

Build the reliable core first

Must have

  • Offline job access and queued updates.
  • Guided photo evidence linked to a job.
  • Exception and variation submission.
  • Operator review queue and recorded decisions.
  • Performance dashboard and audit history.

Should have

  • Four-hour decision timer and escalation.
  • AI-assisted evidence and rate-card checks.
  • Advanced capacity insights.
  • Predictive parts replenishment (later phase).

07 · Success metrics & feedback

Measure job outcomes—and satisfaction on both sides

Operator → Firm feedback

After a job is closed, operator teams rate the firm on work quality, first-time-fix, evidence completeness, safety and SLA adherence. Feedback is shared with the firm with clear reasons and coaching actions.

Firm → Operator feedback

Firms rate the operator on job-scope clarity, site readiness/access, dispatcher communication, speed/fairness of variation decisions and payment experience.

How each benefit estimate should be calculated

Dispute reduction · £450K

Annual disputed additional-work value × expected reduction rate (or avoided claim count × average claim value).

Repeat visits · £795.6K

Avoided repeat visits × fully loaded cost per visit (technician time, travel and materials).

Admin effort · £200K

Hours saved on evidence chasing, reconciliation and review × loaded hourly labour cost.

The supplied business case gives the three annual benefit totals, but not their underlying volumes, unit costs or reduction assumptions. Treat these as proposal estimates until Finance validates the inputs against pilot baselines.
