02 · Problem and opportunity

Fragmented evidence creates avoidable cost

Today

  • Job exceptions shared across calls and messages.
  • Evidence arrives late or is incomplete.
  • Variation claims are difficult to verify.
  • Disputes delay payment and consume operations time.

Opportunity

  • Capture evidence at the point of work.
  • Give both parties visibility into job status.
  • Route exceptions through a consistent review process.
  • Make payment decisions faster and auditable.
03 · Personas

Five people. One connected workflow.

Operator · Regional manager

Owns regional performance

Needs visibility into capacity, job completion, SLA and first-time-fix across firms.

Needs: Regional dashboard and firm scorecards.

Operator · Vendor manager

Resolves claims

Needs complete evidence and quick, consistent variation decisions.

Needs: Review queue, rate-card comparison and audit trail.

Partner · Subcontractor owner

Runs the business

Needs predictable work, fair decisions and timely payment to protect margins.

Needs: Transparent job status, feedback and payment visibility.

Partner · Dispatcher

Coordinates crews

Needs to allocate and track work without duplicate administration.

Needs: Job status and simple exception tracking.

Field · Technician

Completes the job on site

Needs a fast, reliable workflow that works in poor-signal conditions, with minimal typing and clear evidence prompts.

Needs: Mobile job list, guided evidence capture, offline updates and clear job/payment feedback.

04 · Product experience

Two simple product surfaces

Technician mobile app

A job in five steps

  1. Sign in with SMS and OTP.
  2. View assigned jobs and SLA details.
  3. Capture required photos.
  4. Raise a site exception or variation.
  5. Submit now or save offline and sync later.
Operator web console

See work and resolve exceptions

  1. Monitor job, SLA and first-time-fix metrics.
  2. Review requests by urgency.
  3. Compare dispatched scope, claim and evidence.
  4. Approve, modify or reject with a reason.
  5. Track decisions and audit history.

06 · Core requirements

Build the reliable core first

Must have

  • Offline job access and queued updates.
  • Guided photo evidence linked to a job.
  • Exception and variation submission.
  • Operator review queue and recorded decisions.
  • Performance dashboard and audit history.

Should have

  • Four-hour decision timer and escalation.
  • AI-assisted evidence and rate-card checks.
  • Advanced capacity insights.
  • Predictive parts replenishment (later phase).

08 · Two-way feedback

Both sides rate the working experience

Operator → Firm

How well did the firm do?

  • Work quality and first-time fix
  • Evidence completeness and safety
  • SLA adherence
Firm → Operator

How well did the operator support the job?

  • Clear job scope and site access
  • Dispatcher communication
  • Fair decisions and timely payment

Simple feedback loop

1. Rate
Both sides submit a 1–5 rating after job completion. Comments are optional.

2. Review
Keep each direction separate; look for repeated low scores and themes.

3. Improve
Agree an owner and action, then track whether scores improve.

How the benefit estimates are calculated

Dispute reduction · £450K

Avoided disputed claims × average claim value (or disputed value × expected reduction rate).

Repeat visits · £795.6K

Avoided repeat visits × fully loaded cost per visit.

Admin effort · £200K

Hours saved × loaded hourly labour cost.

Benefit inputs are proposal estimates. Validate volumes, unit costs and reduction assumptions against pilot data before committing.

11 · Rollout and decisions

Prove value, then scale by partner volume

Pilot

3 firms · ~150 technicians · ~1,000 jobs/week.

Weeks 7–16

Top partners

25 firms · ~60% of job volume.

Expand

Remaining 175 firms with self-serve onboarding.

Week 27+

Standardize

Digital evidence becomes standard billing path, subject to policy approval.

Decisions before scale

Confirm Day-1 SFTP readiness, four-hour review capacity, payment funding, invoice exceptions and privacy/retention policy.
