Item 2.3 System Architecture

APIs: Systems of Record & 3rd-Party Feeds

Asynchronous, circuit-broken integration designed for legacy telco IT resilience.

Operator Systems of Record (SoR)

Work Orders (WFM / Siebel / Netcracker) PULL / WEBHOOK

Ingests daily dispatch payload. Degraded: Serve cached jobs from Redis; Kafka queue.

Network Inventory / GIS (ESRI / Smallworld) PULL

DP locations and fiber drops. Degraded: Static work order snapshot fallback.

Billing & ERP (SAP S/4HANA / Oracle) PUSH

Pushes approved self-billing milestones. Degraded: Idempotent PostgreSQL outbox queue.

3rd-Party External Integrations

Weather Intelligence (Tomorrow.io) PULL

High winds (>35mph) / storms. Degraded: Auto-allow manual technician weather pause.

Traffic & Routing (Mapbox API) PULL

Driving ETAs & routing. Degraded: Offline Haversine straight-line distance math.

Fiber Technician Certifications PULL

Working at heights & splicing accreditation. Degraded: 7-day grace period on cached status.

Resilience Pattern: Stale-While-Revalidate caching + Circuit Breakers prevent enterprise downtime from freezing field work.
Item 2.4 AI Architecture

Where AI Earns Its Place: Scope Triage

Automating evidence synthesis and contract matching—while keeping financial liability 100% human.

Automated (Model)

Computer Vision & OCR

  • • OCR on Optical Power Meter screen (-18.2 dBm).
  • • Verifies trench depth >300mm from photo.
  • • Perceptual hashing flags duplicate / reused photos.
  • • Maps field condition to Rate Card Code (RC-CIV-08).
  • • Computes confidence score (0–100%).
Human-in-the-Loop

Vendor Manager

  • • Reviews pre-populated diff card in 30 seconds.
  • • Makes 100% of binding financial approval clicks.
  • • Adjudicates subjective or edge-case site conditions.
  • • Modifies rate uplifts or issues counter-offers.
  • • Signs audit-trail event for ERP ledger.
Strict Non-Use

Zero Algorithmic Penalties

  • • NO Automated Clawbacks: Deducting money via AI destroys supplier trust and triggers lawsuits.
  • • NO Algorithmic De-ranking: Stripping vendor work allocation based on black-box scoring breaches labor norms.
  • • NO Auto-Rejection: Low confidence routes to human triage queue, never auto-rejected.
Success Metrics for AI: Triage latency cut by 80% (<90s review) · Human override rate <15% · Dispute recurrence <2%.
Item 2.5 Risk Management

Implementation Challenges & Mitigations

Navigating technical constraints, behavioral inertia, and multi-tenant commercial boundaries.

01 Technical Challenge

24,000 photos/week over congested 2G/3G networks.

Mitigation:

On-device progressive WebP compression (<200KB). Priority metadata sync first, background chunked photo upload queue.

02 Adoption Challenge

Dispatcher inertia ("We just run everything on WhatsApp").

Mitigation:

1-click WhatsApp deep-link invite bridges. Deliver immediate value: instant proof against unfair customer claims.

03 Commercial Challenge

Firms work for rival telcos; fear margin & technician poaching.

Mitigation:

Strict multi-tenant cryptographic partitioning. Operator sees only their own job telemetry; competitor jobs completely invisible.

AI Architecture · Evidence Triage

From Field Evidence to a Human Decision

A clear sequence: validate the capture, analyze evidence, route by confidence, then let a person decide.

01
Capture

Photos, EXIF, GPS and work-order ID

02
Device check

Blur and glare detection before upload

03
AI analysis

Vision/OCR, anti-fraud and rate-card match

04
Decision gate

Confidence ≥85% and delta <£100?

05
Human decision

Vendor manager makes final call

YES · Meets threshold

Pre-populate the difference card

Present the rate-card comparison and evidence for a quick manager review.

NO · Below threshold / mismatch

Assisted review queue

Highlight missing evidence or mismatches for a more detailed human review.

Final step: Vendor manager approves, modifies or rejects the request. The approved rate-card delta is then written to the job record with an audit trail.

The 85% confidence and £100 thresholds are proposed values from the workflow design and require validation before production.
