💡 1. The Story: What is Broken?

Imagine you run a giant broadband company. Every week, people order high-speed internet for their homes. That is 6,000 installs and repairs every week!

You don't employ all the van drivers yourself. Instead, you hire 200 outside contractor companies to do 70% of the physical work.

🛑 The Daily Mess Today:

  1. The Surprise: A technician arrives at a house. The underground pipe is crushed by tree roots. It will take 2 extra hours of digging.
  2. The WhatsApp Chat: The worker takes a blurry photo on WhatsApp and tells his boss, but nobody alerts the big broadband company.
  3. The Clock Keeps Ticking: The system thinks the worker is late and automatically fines them!
  4. The 45-Day Fight: 6 weeks later, a surprise bill arrives for £95 extra. The broadband company says "We didn't approve this, so we won't pay."
  5. The Anger: The contractor company gets furious. They pull their best workers and send them to your competitor instead!

👥 2. Who is Fighting and Why?

Both sides are actually good people who just want to do their jobs, but the current tools make them fight:

🏢 The Operator (The Big Broadband Boss)

  • • What they want: Internet installed fast, zero customer complaints, and no fake surprise charges.
  • • What annoys them: Getting mystery bills 45 days late with blurry photos of dirt.
  • • What makes them say NO: If a tool forces them to re-type data by hand into slow spreadsheets.

🚐 The Subcontractor (The Van Crews)

  • • What they want: Fast, fair payment every week so they can pay their workers on Friday.
  • • What annoys them: Losing money because an app crashes in a basement or fines them for things they didn't cause.
  • • What makes them say NO: If you charge them a fee to use the app, or if it takes >2 minutes per job!
🤝 The Golden Deal:
• The broadband company funds the platform (because it saves them £1M+ in fake bills).
• The app is 100% FREE for all subcontractors and technicians.
• If workers take clear photos on site, they are guaranteed payment in 7 days instead of 45 days.

🛠️ 3. The Solution: Two Simple Tools

We built two simple tools that talk to each other in real-time:

Tool 1: The Field Tech Mobile App (For the Van)

  • ✓ Under 2 Minutes: 4 simple taps on big buttons that work even with thick work gloves. Zero typing required.
  • ✓ Works with ZERO Cell Signal: When a worker is in a basement or rural lane with no 4G, the app saves everything safely on the phone without spinning or crashing.
  • ✓ 3 Quick Guided Photos:
    1. The light meter (the app reads the number automatically: -18.2 dBm).
    2. The neat wire splice tray.
    3. The outside customer wall box.
  • ✓ 1-Tap "Blocked" Button: If a gate is locked or a pipe is broken, tap one button. The timer stops immediately so the worker doesn't get in trouble!

Tool 2: The Operator Web Console (For the Office)

  • ✓ Live Company Scoreboard: Ranks all 200 partner companies. Good performers automatically get more work!
  • ✓ Strict 4-Hour Decision Rule: When a worker asks for an extra payment, the manager has 4 hours to decide. If the manager ignores it, the worker wins automatically!
  • ✓ Side-by-Side Photo Check: See what was ordered on the left, what extra work was done in the middle, and the verified photos on the right. 1-click to approve.

🤖 4. What AI Does (And What It is NEVER Allowed to Do)

We use smart computer vision like a helpful assistant, but never as an angry boss:

✅ What the AI Assistant Does:

  • • Reads the meter numbers from photos automatically.
  • • Checks if trench photos are at least 30cm deep.
  • • Catches duplicate photos if someone reuses a photo from last month.
  • • Matches the extra work to the correct pricing rule.

❌ What AI is NEVER Allowed to Do:

  • • NO AI Deductions: The robot can never take money away from a worker.
  • • NO AI Firing: The robot can never ban or punish a company.
  • • 100% Human Approval: A real human manager must always click the final "Approve Payment" button.

🚀 6. How We Roll It Out (No Chaos)

We don't force 200 companies to switch overnight. We do it in 4 safe steps:

1
Weeks 1–6 (The Friendly Test): Start with 3 friendly companies (150 workers) in one city. Guarantee them 7-day payment. Fix all bugs.
2
Weeks 7–16 (The Big Players): Bring on the 25 biggest companies (which do 60% of all jobs). Train their dispatchers in half-day sessions.
3
Weeks 17–26 (The Small Outfits): Onboard the remaining 175 smaller companies using a 3-minute video guide and an SMS link. No app store download needed.
4
Week 27+ (The Simple Rule): Send verified photos on the app = Get paid in 7 days. Send paper bills = Wait in a 60-day manual audit queue.

🛡️ 7. The 4 Big Worries & Why We Won't Fail

Worry 1: "What if technicians take fake photos from their van window?"

The Fix: The phone checks that the van was parked outside the house for at least 10 minutes, checks GPS coordinates, and blocks photos picked from the phone's saved camera roll.

Worry 2: "What if our old enterprise IT takes 2 years to connect?"

The Fix: We don't wait for IT! On Day 1, we import and export simple scheduled spreadsheet files (CSV via SFTP). It works immediately.

Worry 3: "What if workers strike because they think it's a spy tracker?"

The Fix: Zero background GPS tracking! The app only looks at location for 1 second when the tech taps "Arrived" and "Done". It respects their privacy.

Worry 4: "What if the office gets flooded with 200 requests every day?"

The Fix: Small claims under £25 with high confidence get auto-approved with a 10% random check. Humans only review the ~30 big ones each day.
