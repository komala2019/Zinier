1. Executive Summary & The £50M Blind Spot

A national fiber broadband operator delivers ~6,000 jobs per week, relying on ~200 independent subcontractor firms for 70% of field capacity (residential FTTH installs, customer premise drops, and fault repairs).

Today, the interface between operator and external partners is a complete black box: requests travel over ad-hoc WhatsApp chats and unmonitored inboxes. Variations appear on invoices 45 days later, triggering commercial disputes, delayed payments, and technician defection to rival telecom networks.

3. Product Solution & UX Architecture

The platform consists of two specialized surfaces connected to a synchronized state machine:

Surface 1: Field Tech Mobile App (Android 11+, Offline-First)

  • 6-Step Fast Onboarding (<3 Minutes): SMS deep link → Auto-reads OTP (binds phone to firm) → Transparent camera & location permissions → 12MB offline pack download → 45s skippable tour → Ready for Job 1.
  • Offline as Default State: Built on local encrypted SQLite store. Every tap commits in 0ms without network spinners. Monotonic sequence IDs and GPS UTC stamps prevent duplicate actions.
  • <120-Second Field Budget: 4-tap workflow, 48px+ touch targets for gloved fingers, zero mandatory free-text entry, and a live SLA countdown clock.
  • Guided 3-Photo Closeout:
    1. Optical Power Meter (OPM): On-device OCR verifies numeric reading (-18.2 dBm) within safe optical attenuation limits.
    2. Fusion Splice Tray: Visual check for neat fiber routing and heat shrink protection.
    3. Premise Entry CSP Box: Verifies weatherproof grommet and exterior demarcation.
  • 1-Tap Scope Mismatch Drawer: Submits rate variation or SLA pause with automatically bound 10-minute stationary GPS and UTC timestamp.

Surface 2: Operator Web Console (Next.js / Tailwind)

  • Firm Performance Scorecard: Benchmarks 200 firms in real-time on SLA Hit Rate (%), First-Time Fix (% 30-day rework-free), and Dispute Rate (%). Eliminates 2 days/month of manual analyst work.
  • Strict 4-Hour Decision SLA: Visual countdown on paused jobs. Changes from slate to amber (2h) to red (3.5h). Default Rule: If the operator stalls past 4 hours, the claim automatically credits in the subcontractor's favor.
  • Side-by-Side Adjudication Queue: Dispatched contract scope, claimed variation diff (+£45), and raw high-res geotagged photos displayed adjacent to each other.
  • 1-Click Action Bar: Approve (£45), Counter-Offer / Modify, Request More Info, or Reject with mandatory audit reason codes (EVID-INSUFFICIENT, OUT-OF-SCOPE-PERMIT).

4. System Architecture & AI Governance

Decoupled Resilience & Degraded Modes

National telco IT is notoriously slow to integrate. The platform decouples launch from legacy enterprise IT schedules:

System Endpoint Integration Data Payload Degraded Mode Behavior (Downtime Fallback)
Operator WFM (Siebel / Netcracker) PULL / Webhook Daily work orders, target SLA, scope code. Circuit Breaker: Serve cached jobs from Redis/Postgres; buffer updates in Kafka.
Operator GIS (ESRI / Smallworld) PULL / REST DP location, duct availability, fiber pair. Static Snapshot: Uses pre-cached dispatch snapshot; flags pending check.
Operator Billing (SAP S/4HANA) PUSH / Webhook Approved completion tokens, self-billing advice. PostgreSQL Outbox: Resilient outbox retries with idempotency keys.
Weather Feed (Tomorrow.io) PULL / Hourly Winds >35mph, lightning alerts. Permissive Fallback: Auto-allows manual tech pause; flags for spot review.

*Day 1 Pragmatic Ingestion: On Day 1, we ingest via scheduled SFTP flat-file CSV batch drops, avoiding 12-month telco IT API development delays.

AI Workflow: Scope Triage & Rate Variations

Computer vision and LLMs synthesize evidence—while keeping financial liability 100% in human hands.

What AI Decides (Model)
  • • OCR reads -18.2 dBm on OPM display.
  • • Validates trench depth >300mm.
  • • Perceptual hash detects duplicate/reused photos.
  • • Maps condition to contract code (RC-CIV-04).
  • • Generates confidence score (0–100%).
What Human Decides (Approver)
  • • Reviews pre-populated diff in <30s.
  • • Executes 100% of binding financial approval clicks.
  • • Adjudicates ambiguous edge cases.
  • • Modifies rate uplifts or issues counter-offers.
  • • Signs audit-trail event for ERP ledger.
Where AI is Forbidden
  • • NO Automated Clawbacks: Machine deductions destroy trust and trigger litigation.
  • • NO Algorithmic De-ranking: Banning vendors via black-box scoring breaches labor norms.
  • • NO Auto-Rejection: Low confidence routes to human queue, never rejected.

5. Business Model, Unit Economics & 2.86x ROI

The Subcontractor Margin Law

Subcontractors operate on paper-thin 5% to 8% net profit margins. Charging them a 2% software fee wipes out 25% to 40% of their net profit, triggering workforce defection. The core platform is 100% FREE to subcontractors and technicians.

Monetization Structure

  • Operator Enterprise SaaS: £1.20 per completed job (funded by Operator) = £374,400 ARR across 312,000 jobs/year.
  • Optional FinTech Fast-Pay: Standard Net-45 settlement is always free. Optional 48-hour settlement at 1.5% dynamic discount factoring funded by banking partner.

6. Go-To-Market Rollout & Strategic Roadmap

4-Phase GTM Strategy (Scaling to 200 Firms in 26 Weeks)

Phase 1: Pilot (W1–6)

3 Tier-1 firms (~150 techs, 1,000 jobs/wk) in Northern region. Validates offline sync and <2 min budget.
Carrot: Guaranteed Net-7 pay.

Phase 2: Top 25 (W7–16)

Top 25 firms delivering 60% of total weekly volume. Dedicated train-the-dispatcher workshops; dispatcher portal live.

Phase 3: Long-Tail (W17–26)

Remaining 175 small outfits (3–15 techs). Self-serve onboarding via SMS deep link and 3-min video guides.

Phase 4: Mandate (W27+)

Billing policy update: unverified paper invoices face 60-day audit queues; app-verified pays in 7 days.

18-Month Strategic Horizons

  • Horizon 1 (M1–3) — Core Loop: Offline mobile app, 4-hour operator console, rate card engine, SFTP flat-file batch drops.
  • Horizon 2 (M4–9) — Smart Field Operations: AI evidence OCR, predictive van stock replenishment via distributor APIs (Rexel, Graybar), automated radar weather delay shield (>35mph wind mass SLA pause).
  • Horizon 3 (M10–18) — Network Ecosystem: Peer-to-peer van parts exchange between non-competing crews via QR scan, predictive rework prevention, and collective purchasing consortium for van leasing, fuel cards, and fusion splicers.

7. Live Roleplays: COO Pitch & Scathing Self-Critique

7.1 3-Minute COO Executive Pitch Script

"Good morning. You run 6,000 fiber jobs every week, with 70% delivered by 200 external partner firms. Right now, that £50 million field operation is managed through a complete blind spot between 8:00 AM dispatch and a disputed invoice arriving 45 days later.

When a technician finds a collapsed duct or a customer no-show, requests travel over WhatsApp and email. The SLA clock keeps running, deductions feel arbitrary, and subcontractors reassign their top certified crews to your direct competitors who pay faster.

We sit cleanly between your enterprise systems and the van without ripping and replacing your IT. Technicians get an offline-first app that captures geotagged optical power proof in under 90 seconds. Variations become same-day decisions under a strict 4-hour SLA. In return, you guarantee verified payment in 7 to 14 days, securing your labor supply.

The Economics: £1.20 per completed job equals £374k annual platform cost. In return, you recover £1.07 million in net savings by cutting dispute leakage and avoiding 9,360 second-truck rolls. That is a 2.86x ROI in year one. Let's launch the 6-week pilot in the Northern region."

7.2 Scathing Self-Critique & Defensive Countermeasures

Brutal Failure Mode (Critique) Defensive Countermeasure
1. Evidence Fraud:
Techs will snap random closed gates from vans to falsely pause SLA clocks.		 Triple-Vector Telemetry Binding: Hardware camera EXIF (blocks gallery uploads) + 10-minute continuous stationary GPS within 30m boundary + OPM OCR validation. Statistical outlier clustering flags abuse patterns.
2. Telco IT Integration Graveyard:
Waiting 18 months for legacy SOAP/WFM APIs will kill the pilot.		 Decoupled Asynchronous SFTP Architecture: Day 1 runs on scheduled flat-file CSV batch drops of jobs and self-billing remittance outputs. Zero synchronous API dependency for launch.
3. Surveillance Backlash:
Techs will strike over an 'electronic ankle monitor' tracking their movement.		 Privacy-by-Design: Zero continuous background GPS tracking; location polled strictly at arrival and closeout taps. Bi-directional transparency: techs track the operator's decision and payment SLA clocks.
4. Vendor Manager Queue Overload:
200 daily variation requests will break the 4-hour decision SLA.		 Tiered Algorithmic Auto-Clearance: Micro-variations (<£25 with >95% AI confidence) auto-approve with a 10% spot audit. Active human queue drops from 200/day to fewer than 35 critical decisions per day.
